How a daily round works

1 Set your prices Enter a price per model and state in the Price manager, or import a CSV.
2 Before 16:00 CET Prices saved before 16:00 count for that day. Bids are sealed: nobody sees anyone else’s price.
3 Highest price wins Each device goes to the buyer with the highest price for its model and state. The next day you see yesterday’s best price.
4 Assessment and shipping Won devices are checked at an assessment centre, then shipped to you and invoiced.

Device states

Every device is checked for activation lock and blacklist status. Data is wiped where possible.
